Notes and conditions

Important information for planning your appointment or celebration.

  1. A deposit of 50% of the total invoice is required to reserve your date. This deposit is non-refundable if you cancel.
  2. If you choose one of our plans, the full balance must be paid when you attend the makeup trial. Your wedding date is then reserved.
  3. Payment is accepted in Dominican pesos or US dollars, in cash or by bank transfer.
  4. For services outside La Romana, travel and accommodation expenses are added to your invoice.
  5. Please arrive on time for your appointment.
  6. The wedding planner should provide us with the established event schedule.
  7. Hair must be clean and dry. Washing is not included.
  8. Please let your bridesmaids and family know that any additional hairstyling or makeup service has an extra charge.

About me

Beauty is my inspiration

Behind ArteVital Studio & Esthetic is a vision of beauty that brings together artistry, aesthetics and personal attention.

I am Jina Morales, the person behind ArteVital, a beauty and aesthetics studio in La Romana, Dominican Republic, where professional makeup, hairstyling and aesthetic care come together in experiences designed for each person.

At ArteVital, we believe true beauty begins when you feel good about yourself. Our approach goes beyond a treatment or a new look: we want every detail of your experience to bring out your natural beauty.

Our philosophy is inspired by three pillars: beauty, harmony and wellness. From makeup for a special occasion to a hairstyle or an aesthetic treatment, we want every visit to be a moment dedicated entirely to you.

ArteVital was born from an identity that sees aesthetics as a form of personal expression, where every face, style and occasion deserves thoughtful attention.

I invite you to discover ArteVital, a space created for you to enjoy personal care and find new ways to express your beauty.

Jina Morales · ArteVital Studio & Esthetic · La Romana, Dominican Republic. Your beauty, our inspiration.
